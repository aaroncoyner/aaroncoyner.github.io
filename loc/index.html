💀 The blame ledger

When a ticket dies, everyone who missed shares the blame equally. Each dead ticket's dream payout (stake × odds) is split among that week's missers. A person's total is the sum of their shares. Example: week 4 would have paid $21,807 and 4 people missed, so each is charged $5,452.
